Activities

Hikes

The Crack Moderate 6 km return 3-4 hrs

Iconic Killarney hike — scramble through a crack in the quartzite ridge with views of Georgian Bay

Granite Ridge Trail Easy 2 km loop 1 hr

Short trail with great panoramic views of the La Cloche Mountains

Cranberry Bog Trail Easy 4 km loop 1.5 hrs

Boardwalk trail through a floating bog — unique ecosystem

Chikanishing Trail Easy-Moderate 3 km loop 1.5 hrs

Beautiful Georgian Bay shoreline walk over smooth granite

Swimming

  • George Lake beach (at campground)
  • Topaz Lake (accessed via The Crack trail)

Paddling

  • George Lake — easy paddle, canoe/kayak rentals available
  • Killarney Lake — stunning turquoise water, portage required from George Lake

Tips

  • The Crack parking lot fills up early in summer — go before 9 AM
  • Bugs are intense in June/early July, bring DEET or permethrin
  • Park store at George Lake has firewood, ice, basic supplies
  • Cell service is spotty — download offline maps
  • Nearest full grocery: Killarney village (20 min from park gate)
