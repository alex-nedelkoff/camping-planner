# Camping Planner — Product Context

## Product purpose
A private trip-planning app for a small group of friends to plan canoe and
car-camping trips: parks and access points, paddle/drive routes, gear and food
lists, per-person checklists, and shared notes. Not a commercial product; it's a
shared tool for one crew.

## Users
A handful of friends who plan multi-day Ontario backcountry and car-camping
trips together. Comfortable outdoors, not necessarily technical. Access is gated
by one shared site password; each person signs in with a free-text name.

## Register
- **brand** — marketing / welcome surfaces (the login splash). Design *is* the welcome.
- **product** — the app itself (trip pages, gear, checklists, feedback). Design *serves* the task.

## Brand voice
Three words: **warm, analog, cartographic.** Like a well-kept field journal or a
topographic map: hand-made, unfussy, a little adventurous. Confident but not loud.

## Tone (copy)
Friendly and concise. A little trail humor is welcome. No corporate filler, no
restated headings, no em dashes.

## Anti-references (avoid)
Generic SaaS dashboards; neon-on-black "tech"; glassmorphism; gradient text;
editorial-magazine serif clichés (display-italic + drop caps + ruled columns);
stock "adventure brand" gradients. Average reads as nobody made it.

## Strategic principles
- The visual identity is already established (parchment field-journal palette +
  Fraunces display). **Preserve it** rather than chase trends; variants extend it.
- In the app (product register), clarity and speed beat decoration.
- On the splash (brand register), make the front door feel like the start of a trip.
